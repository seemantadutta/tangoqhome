Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official Mixxx?

No. TangoQ is an independent, unofficial fork of Mixxx - not affiliated with or endorsed by the Mixxx project. It is built on Mixxx and released under the same GPL license; the official app lives at mixxx.org.

Why not just use another DJ app like djay Pro, Traktor or VirtualDJ?

Those apps are built for club DJs who beat-match and blend one track into the next. None of them understand a cortina, or that at a milonga every track stands on its own with no blending between them. TangoQ is built around exactly that tango-specific handling.

Is it still Mixxx underneath? Do I lose anything?

Yes, it is Mixxx under the hood (version 2.5.6), so your music library, track analysis, controller mappings and settings all work just as they do in the official build. TangoQ adds a tango cockpit on top; it doesn't take Mixxx away.

Will my existing library, tracks and controllers work?

Yes. On first launch TangoQ can import your existing Mixxx library into its own separate database, so your music, analysis, mappings and settings come across - and your original Mixxx setup is never modified.

Why does my computer warn me about the installer? Is it safe?

Because these builds are not code-signed (that needs a paid developer certificate this project does not have), Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper flag them as coming from an unknown developer. It is not malware: the full source is public and you can build it yourself. Full step-by-step instructions for getting past the warning come with the download.

Which platforms does it run on?

Windows and macOS. Linux users can build from source for now; packaged Linux builds may follow.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source under the GNU GPL. If it earns a place in your booth you are welcome to chip in via the donate link, but that is entirely optional.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Open an issue on the fork's GitHub via the Report a bug link. Reports that include your OS and the steps to reproduce are the most helpful.
